The board runs wide to tight and back out: 01 sets the place and her scale in it, 02 walks her to the aeroplane, 03 and 04 are the checks before a flight, hands on the gear and eyes on the map, 05 and 06 bring the camera to her face and then to the goggles, and 07 and 08 put her in the cockpit and look up with her. The reference carried the face. The prompt held everything else: the flight cap, the shearling jacket, the breeches and boots, and the light and lens for each shot. 03 came back outdoors at sunset and was kept, because the sequence is about her surviving the cut, not the set staying put.

ModelQwen-Image-Edit 2511 in ComfyUI with the Qwen 2.5-VL text encoder. Shot 08 is a four-step Lightning pass of the same model.
IdentityHeld by the model from the reference image, with a prompt per shot.
ChecksEvery frame checked against the reference by score and by eye, two encoders under each frame. Three shots needed help and are marked.
CharacterSynthetic throughout. The reference image was itself generated.

Outfit down, lighting across, so one axis holds while the other moves: the same face in three keys, warm dusk with a cool rim, open morning sun and tungsten in deep shadow, and in three wardrobes that change the hair and the framing with them.

She holds through the jacket and the gown. The coveralls row is where she slips: the kerchief changes how the face is framed, and the two cells that read least like her are both there.
